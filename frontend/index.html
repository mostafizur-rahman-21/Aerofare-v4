AeroFare — Architecture & Viva Guide

Computer Science & Engineering Project • Team K

Project Problem Statement & Goal

Domestic flight ticket prices fluctuate dynamically based on demand, lead time, and route factors. AeroFare estimates fair historical ticket prices using Machine Learning and compares them with real-time market quotes to alert travelers about price surges.
